# Spending Insights

The **Spending Insights** page is currently under construction.

It is intended to provide additional analysis of household spending patterns.

Until this page is completed, use the existing report pages for spending analysis.

## Income & Expenses

Use **Income & Expenses** to review:

- monthly spending amounts;
- category trends;
- total expenses;
- net savings.

## Income & Expenses: % of Total Income

Use this view to understand how much of household income is being consumed by each category.

## Actual vs Budget

Use **Actual vs Budget** to identify:

- categories above or below budget;
- dollar differences;
- percentage differences;
- the effect on overall savings.

## Category Detail

Use **Category Detail** when you need to investigate the activity behind a particular income or expense category.

## Cash Flow

Use **Cash Flow** when the question is not simply how much was spent, but how spending and other cash movements affect available bank cash.

## Current status

No conclusions should be drawn from incomplete Spending Insights visuals until the page has been fully implemented and validated.
