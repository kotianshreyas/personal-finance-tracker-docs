# Portfolio Holdings

The **Portfolio Holdings** page provides the detailed investment list behind the portfolio.

It is useful for answering questions such as:

1. What investments do I currently own?
2. What is each investment worth?
3. How much investment cost remains associated with each holding?
4. How much income has each investment generated?
5. How much gain or loss has already been realized?
6. How much gain or loss remains unrealized?
7. Which investments have produced the strongest or weakest results?

Values are shown in Canadian dollars unless otherwise indicated.

## Holdings table

Each row represents an investment.

### Symbol

The investment's ticker symbol or other short identifier.

### Name

The full name of the investment.

### Qty

The number of units or shares currently held.

### Price

The price currently being used to value one unit or share.

When current market pricing is available, this can reflect a recently refreshed market price.

### Mkt. Value

The estimated current value of the investment.

In simple terms:

**Market Value = Quantity × Price**

### Income

Investment income received from the holding, such as dividends, distributions, or interest where applicable.

Income is different from a gain caused by the investment price increasing.

### Realized

Gain or loss already recognized from units or shares that have been sold.

Once part of an investment has been sold, the result on that sold portion is no longer merely an unrealized change in market value.

### Unrealized

Gain or loss on the portion of the investment that is still owned.

This amount changes as the current price changes.

### Total Gain

The combined economic gain associated with the investment in the report.

It brings together the relevant investment gain and income components so that the overall result can be reviewed in one figure.

### Remaining Cost

The remaining investment cost associated with the units or shares that are still owned.

Comparing **Remaining Cost** with **Mkt. Value** helps show how much the current holding has increased or decreased in value.

### Gain %

Shows the overall investment gain or loss relative to its investment cost.

A positive percentage indicates a gain and a negative percentage indicates a loss.

### Ann. Return

**Annualized Return** expresses investment performance as an annual rate.

This is useful when comparing investments that have been held for different lengths of time.

For example, two investments may both show a 20% gain, but one may have taken several years to achieve it while another achieved it much faster. Annualized Return provides additional context for that comparison.

It should therefore be considered together with the age of the holding rather than on its own.

## Total row

The bottom row summarizes the holdings currently included in the page.

It can include totals for:

- quantity;
- market value;
- investment income;
- realized gain or loss;
- unrealized gain or loss;
- total gain;
- remaining cost;
- overall Gain %.

These totals change when filters are applied.

## Filters

### Date

Controls the reporting period.

**Latest** shows the most recent available transaction position.

### Holding Status

Allows the page to focus on holdings based on their current status.

For example, the standard view can be restricted to currently active holdings.

### Account Name

Shows investments belonging to a selected investment account.

### Financial Institution

Filters holdings by the institution where they are held.

### Owner

Filters holdings by account owner.

### Purpose

Filters holdings according to the financial purpose assigned to the account or investment.

Examples can include:

- retirement;
- home buying;
- education;
- other financial goals.

### Gain / Loss

Allows investments to be filtered according to whether they currently show a gain or a loss.

## Current price information

The upper-right corner shows when live prices were most recently refreshed.

Not every investment has the same type of market-price availability.

Some investments may use a periodically updated price rather than a continuously changing market price. This can result in less frequent price changes even though the investment remains correctly included in the portfolio.

## Reset

Select **Reset** to clear page selections and return to the normal starting view.

## Practical uses

### Find the largest investments

Review **Mkt. Value** to identify which holdings represent the greatest dollar exposure.

### Find the strongest overall results

Review both:

- **Total Gain** for the dollar result;
- **Gain %** for the result relative to investment cost.

### Compare investments held for different periods

Use **Ann. Return** together with the **Portfolio Analysis** page.

A larger total gain does not automatically mean an investment performed better if it was held for much longer.

### Review one account

Use **Account Name**, **Owner**, or **Financial Institution** to isolate the relevant account.

### Investigate a losing investment

Locate negative Gain % values or use the Gain / Loss filter.

Then use **Portfolio Analysis** to add information about:

- portfolio weight;
- holding age;
- recent price trend;
- review status.

## Relationship with other pages

Use **Investment Overview** when you want to understand the overall structure of the investment portfolio.

Use **Portfolio Analysis** when you want to investigate whether particular holdings deserve closer attention.

Use **Holding Breakdown** when you want account-level detail behind one selected investment.

## Key question answered

> **What exactly do I own, what is it worth, and how has each investment performed?**
