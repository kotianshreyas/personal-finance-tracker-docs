# Holding Breakdown

The **Holding Breakdown** page provides additional detail for an individual investment selected from the portfolio.

It is useful when one investment is held in more than one account or when you want to understand what sits behind the portfolio-level result.

## What this page helps answer

1. In which accounts is this investment held?
2. How much of the investment belongs to each account?
3. What is the value of each account's portion?
4. How much remaining cost belongs to each account?
5. How are gains, income, and other results divided across the accounts?

## Opening the page

Where drill-through is available:

1. Select the investment you want to investigate.
2. Open the available drill-through menu.
3. Choose **Holding Breakdown**.
4. The page opens already filtered to the selected investment.

The exact Power BI menu wording can vary slightly depending on how the report is being viewed.

## Current Holding

The page title identifies the selected investment and indicates that the information relates to the current holding.

This helps distinguish the investment itself from the individual accounts in which it is held.

## Account-level detail

The table breaks the selected investment into its underlying accounts.

This is particularly useful when the same investment is held in more than one account.

For example, the same security might be held partly in an FHSA and partly in a TFSA.

At the total portfolio level those holdings can appear as one investment, while the Holding Breakdown page shows how the position is divided between the accounts.

## Market Value

Shows the value of the selected investment attributable to each account.

## Remaining Cost

Shows the remaining investment cost associated with the units still held in each account.

## Realized Gain

Shows gain or loss already associated with units sold from the relevant account where applicable.

## Unrealized Gain

Shows gain or loss on the units that are still held in the account.

## Income

Shows relevant investment income associated with the account.

## Total Gain

Provides an overall gain figure for the selected investment within the account.

## Gain %

Shows the gain or loss relative to the investment cost associated with that account.

## Why account-level detail matters

The portfolio-level result can sometimes hide meaningful differences between accounts.

For example:

- the same investment may have been purchased at different times;
- different accounts may have different remaining costs;
- sales may have occurred in one account but not another;
- one account may hold substantially more units than another.

The Holding Breakdown page makes those differences visible.

## Returning to the portfolio

Use the report's Back or Home navigation to leave the drill-through view and return to the main report.

## Relationship with other pages

Use **Portfolio Holdings** for the consolidated investment result.

Use **Portfolio Analysis** to evaluate the investment's portfolio importance and current performance context.

Use **Holding Breakdown** when you want to understand the accounts behind that result.

## Key question answered

> **How is this investment divided across my accounts, and what is behind its overall result?**
