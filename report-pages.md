# Report Pages

The Personal Finance Tracker is divided into report pages, with each page designed to answer a different financial question.

## Portfolio Holdings

Shows individual investments, their current values, investment costs, income, gains or losses, and performance.

## Holding Breakdown

Provides account-level detail behind a selected investment.

## Portfolio Analysis

Helps identify investments that may deserve attention by combining portfolio weight, holding age, investment performance, and recent price behaviour.

## Investment Overview

Shows how investment assets are organized by institution, account, purpose, and holding.

## Balance Sheet

Shows assets, liabilities, net worth, financial goals, liquidity, and long-term financial position.

## Cash Flow

Shows recent and projected cash movements and expected bank balances.

## Income & Expenses

Shows monthly income, expenses, and savings in dollar amounts.

## Income & Expenses: % of Total Income

Shows the same financial activity relative to monthly income.

## Actual vs Budget

Compares actual financial activity with planned budget amounts.

## Category Detail

Provides additional detail behind a selected income or expense category.

## Spending Insights

Currently under construction.

## Contribution Tracker

Tracks registered-plan contributions, withdrawals, and remaining contribution room.

Select a page from the navigation menu to learn how to read and use it.
